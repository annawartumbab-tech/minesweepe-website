Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Minesweeper (package name com.Minesweeper.TopGame) handles information when you use the app.

Privacy at a glance

According to the app developer, Minesweeper does not collect, transmit, or share users’ personal data.

1. Information we collect

Minesweeper does not collect personal information or other user data from the app. The developer does not request or receive names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise locations, contacts, photos, or other personal information through the app.

2. How information is used

Because the app does not collect user data, the developer does not use such data for advertising, analytics, profiling, marketing, or any other purpose.

3. Data sharing and transfers

The app does not transmit user data to the developer or share user data with third parties. The developer does not sell user information.

4. Data storage, retention, and deletion

The developer does not maintain a database of user information collected through the app, and therefore has no collected user data to retain or delete. If you have a question about information on your device, you can contact us using the address below.

5. Children’s privacy

Minesweeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy concern, please contact us.

6. Security

Since the app does not collect or transmit user data to the developer, the developer does not store personal data on its servers. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Third-party services

The developer states that the app does not transmit or share user data with third parties. The Google Play Store and the Android operating system may process information independently under their own privacy policies when you download, install, or use apps. This policy describes the app developer’s data practices, not Google’s independent services.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app’s functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page, together with a revised “Last updated” date. If data practices change, the policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact us

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact: support@minesweepegame.com.
